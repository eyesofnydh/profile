02 / NOTES FROM THE ROAD

More than a place.

Open a chapter.
Stay a little longer.

Munnar / June 2025

Some mornings are worth getting lost in.

↗

We left before the town woke up. Somewhere along the climb, the road disappeared into a cloud.

There was no grand plan for the morning. Just a winding road, a flask of tea, and enough time to stop whenever the view asked us to.

By afternoon the mist had lifted. The hills looked completely different, but it was that first quiet hour that stayed with me.

Along the way

Slow drives, short walks, tea stops. A light layer for the early mornings.

The part I kept

For a few minutes, the mountains disappeared and the whole road belonged to the mist.
Back to the places ↑
Varkala / September 2025

We stayed until the sky ran out of color.

↗

The afternoon was meant to be a short stop by the sea. It became the whole day.

We walked without checking the time, stopping for something cold and finding our way back to the water. The conversations moved at the same pace as the waves.

At sunset, everyone seemed to pause together. I took a few photographs, then put the camera away.

Along the way

A coastal weekend with long walks and an unhurried evening. Carry water and leave room in the day for doing very little.

The part I kept

We forgot what we were talking about when the sea turned pink.
Back to the places ↑
Fort Kochi / January 2026

A different story around every corner.

↗

I went looking for a change of scene and found a reason to take the long way around.

The best part of the day was not a place on a list. It was a conversation outside a small shop, followed by a street I had almost walked past.

We stayed out as the lights came on. The city softened into blue, and familiar things started to look new.

Along the way

A day on foot, a few café stops, and an evening by the water. Comfortable shoes matter more than a busy itinerary.

The part I kept

A stranger's directions became the best detour of the day.
