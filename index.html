A DIFFERENT WAY TO SEE

Take a little inspiration with you.

01 / LIGHT

Chase the quiet hours

Look for soft light early in the morning or near sunset. Notice how shadows change the mood of a familiar place.

02 / COMPOSITION

Leave room for the story

Try a lower viewpoint, a natural frame, or a little negative space. One small shift can change the whole photograph.

03 / CURIOSITY

Find wonder close to home

Pick one familiar street and look for a detail you usually miss: a reflection, a texture, or a fleeting expression.
